**What that means**

1. For broad queries, **directories and "top 10" listicles hold the top spots**. A single clinic site will rarely beat them for "دندانپزشک شیراز". Instead, (a) get listed on those directories, which also gives backlinks, and (b) win the narrower queries below.
2. **Winnable first**: area plus service (`دندانپزشک زیبایی معالی آباد`, `لمینت معالی آباد`, `کامپوزیت معالی آباد`) and **price/installment** queries (`قیمت لمینت دندان شیراز ۱۴۰۵`, `لمینت اقساطی شیراز`). The clinic offers installments, so this fits.
3. **Map pack and "near me"** depend on Google Business Profile, not the website. This is the highest-value action.
4. The home page is indexed and indexable, and its title now targets "دندانپزشک زیبایی در شیراز، معالی‌آباد".

**Limits of this analysis**: I had no keyword-volume data. The `competition_guess` column is judged from the results above, not measured. Replace it with Search Console data after about 4 weeks. Real queries and impressions beat any estimate.

## 4. Local SEO (highest impact)

1. **Google Business Profile**: claim and verify. Categories "Cosmetic dentist" and "Dentist". Add services, hours, photos, booking link, and the same name/address/phone as the site. Post weekly. Answer Q&A.
2. **Reviews**: ask every satisfied patient (QR code at the front desk, SMS after the visit). Reply to all. Never buy or fake reviews.
3. **Neshan and Balad** listings with the same details.
4. **NAP consistency**: one exact address string everywhere. Use the version on the site footer.
5. **Dentist schema upgrade**: add `geo`, `hasMap` (the GBP link), `logo`, `priceRange`, and the GBP URL in `sameAs`.
6. **Area page** for Maaliabad with the real address, landmarks, parking and transit directions, and its own FAQ.

## 5. On-page and content

- Add to each service page: process, who it suits, risks and limits, aftercare, installment info, 6 or more FAQs written as real patient questions, and one real before/after case with consent.
- **Price pages** are a major win in these results. State what drives price (number of teeth, material, case complexity) and give honest ranges only if the clinic confirms them. Update the year in the title each Farvardin.
- **About page**: add education, training and memberships, and explain the Fatemeh/Neda name overlap, which currently splits the entity between the site and Instagram.
- **Titles**: keep the primary keyword first, under about 60 characters, with "شیراز" and the brand.
- **Internal links**: every article links to its service page and to `/booking`; every service page links to the 2 or 3 most relevant articles.
- Keep the "reviewed by Dr. Jafari, last reviewed date" block on all medical pages. It supports E-E-A-T.

## 6. AEO / GEO (answer engines)

- Keep FAQ blocks with short, direct 1-2 sentence answers at the top of each answer, then detail below. Keep `FAQPage` schema in sync with the visible text.
- Keep `llms.txt` current (new pages, price pages, area page). Keep AI crawlers allowed in `robots.txt`.
- Entity consistency: same name, license number, address and phone on the site, GBP, directories and Instagram.
- Third-party mentions drive AI answers: directory profiles, listicle inclusions, reviews and media quotes matter more than on-site changes.
- Monthly: run the prompts in `ai-visibility-prompts.csv` on ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude. Log whether the clinic is mentioned, its position, and whether the details are accurate.

## 7. Backlinks

Full list in `backlinks.csv`. In order:

1. Directory and booking platforms: doctoreto, nobat.ir, paziresh24, doctor-yab. They rank for your terms and will send patients.
2. The Medical Council registry profile (trust signal).
3. Listicle sites that rank for "بهترین ... شیراز": request free inclusion with real case photos. Any paid placement must use `rel="sponsored"` or `nofollow`.
4. Local media and expert quotes; local sponsorships; guest articles on Persian health sites.
5. Video on Aparat/YouTube, embedded on the service pages.

**Do not**: buy link packages, use PBNs, or mass-submit to directories. That risks a manual action.

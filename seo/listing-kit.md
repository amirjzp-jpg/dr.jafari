## 4. Instagram: turn it into site traffic

- **Bio link:** `https://dandanpezeshkishiraz.ir/booking`, or the home page. Put "dandanpezeshkishiraz.ir" in the bio text as well, so people type and search the name.
- **Bio text:** «دندانپزشکی زیبایی شیراز | کامپوزیت · لمینت · طراحی لبخند | معالی‌آباد | رزرو آنلاین 👇»
- **Highlights:** «نوبت» (how to book online), «آدرس» (the address and a screenshot of the map), «نمونه‌کار».
- **Every post caption:** end with «رزرو آنلاین: dandanpezeshkishiraz.ir». Use a story link sticker to `/booking` or to the matching page (`/composite`, `/veneers`).
- **Same name and address** as section 1 in the profile's contact details.
- Instagram links are nofollow, so they don't pass ranking directly. They bring visitors and brand searches, and they help Google and AI tools connect the clinic to the site.

## 5. Never

Buying link packages, link networks, mass directory submissions or fake reviews. These risk a Google penalty on a domain this good.
